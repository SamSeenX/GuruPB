CORE METHODOLOGY

The Universal 4-Stage Guru Funnel

Regardless of whether the pitch involves dropshipping, social media agencies, crypto tokens, or AI challenges, the underlying funnel architecture remains unchanged.

STAGE 01 • THE HOOK

The Visual Bait

Rented penthouses, luxury supercars, and exotic destinations create status intimidation. The narrative pits viewers against corporate employment while offering a frictionless escape route.

STAGE 02 • THE SHOVEL

Selling the Manual

Gurus stop competing in the actual commercial market and compete for attention. Freely available YouTube knowledge is repackaged behind high-production paywalls as a proprietary blueprint.

STAGE 03 • THE TRAP

Micro-Upsells & Backend Escalation

Free 5-day challenges require minor fees for VIP tickets or essential workflow software. The entire experience primes participants for high-ticket annual communities costing thousands.

STAGE 04 • THE SHIELD

Deflection of Blame

Zero refund terms and strict legal disclaimers shift complete liability onto the buyer. Failure is blamed on poor work ethic or incorrect mindset while the guru keeps the subscription revenue.
