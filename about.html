About & Research Methodology

Documenting digital funnels through verifiable evidence, legal records, and structural analysis.

The modern internet economy is saturated with high-ticket sales machines promising financial freedom. While some legitimate educators provide structured vocational skills, a massive segment of the industry relies on predatory marketing pipelines, misleading income representations, and psychological coercion.

GuruPB functions as an open public education archive. Our goal is not sensationalism, but arming consumers with the analytical tools needed to recognize predatory sales structures before financial damage occurs.

Investigative Standards

Every case study published in our repository adheres to strict evidentiary protocols:

  • Primary Source Documentation: We evaluate archived landing pages, recorded promotional webinars, live checkout screens, and user terms of service agreements.
  • Public Filings & Disclosures: We cross-reference corporate claims with regulatory enforcement actions from entities like the FTC, SEC, or state attorneys general, alongside corporate registry filings.
  • Structural Rather Than Personal: We dissect the mechanical funnel (the bait, the micro-upsell, the community gate, the blame-deflecting clause) rather than attacking personalities.

The Gray Area Problem

The vast majority of modern course sellers do not run outright illegal schemes that attract immediate federal wire fraud indictments. Instead, they operate inside a sophisticated legal gray zone:

  • They deliver a technical digital file (such as a video library or community invite link) to fulfill transaction obligations.
  • They insert strict non-refundable terms that strip cardholder dispute leverage.
  • They construct community culture where questioning the course value results in immediate expulsion or peer ostracism.

By exposing how these patterns repeat across dropshipping, agency work, crypto coins, and AI tools, GuruPB helps the public spot the pattern before entering the funnel.
