# Scope & Feature Boundary: Scam Guru Playbook

## Current Scope (v1.1.0)
- Zero-Hydration static site generated via VanillaSSG
- Zen educational layout with warm paper palette and annotated syllabus structure
- Interactive Red-Flag Evaluator: Client-side manipulation appraisal tool assessing marketing triggers
- Landing page with 4-stage funnel breakdown and red-flags table
- Investigative Dossiers:
  - Iman Gadzhi: SMMA to AI Funnel Deconstruction
  - Tai Lopez: The Prototype of the Modern Lifestyle Guru
  - The Anatomy of a Modern Guru Funnel
  - The Pivot Cycle: Why Gurus Hop Every 18 Months
- About page with research methodology and evidentiary standards
- Dynamic XML sitemap, RSS feed, robots.txt, and vector OpenGraph cards
- Light/Dark theme switching with persistent local state

## Active Backlog (Future Scope)
- **Searchable Dossiers Directory**: Instant client-side tag filtering by business model (SMMA, AI, Dropshipping, Web3, Real Estate, Affiliate Marketing).
- **Public Evidence Vault**: Downloadable archives of public filings, FTC orders, and archived landing page captures.
- **Whistleblower / Victim Submission Form**: Encrypted or anonymized intake pipeline for course buyer contract terms and upsell records.

## Out of Scope
- User account creation, personal profiles, or paywalls (Scam Guru Playbook remains 100% free and open public education).
- Unverified gossip, personal relationship drama, or unsubstantiated accusations lacking commercial or legal relevance.
