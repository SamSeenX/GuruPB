Changelog: Scam Guru Playbook

All notable changes to the Scam Guru Playbook project are documented in this file.

---

[1.1.0] - 2026-11-03

Added

  • Interactive Red-Flag Evaluator: Built a lightweight Vanilla JS calculator (src/core/evaluator.js and src/styles/evaluator.css) allowing users to check observed marketing patterns and compute an objective 5-tier funnel manipulation index.
  • Accessible Touch Ergonomics: Styled interactive evaluation inputs to meet 48px touch targets with keyboard navigation and focus rings.
  • Global Error Logging: Added resilient window.onerror and window.onunhandledrejection handlers in src/main.js.

---

[1.0.1] - 2026-11-03

Changed

  • Branding Update: Formally adopted the name Scam Guru Playbook across page metadata, headings, navigation landmarks, and OpenGraph assets.
  • Zen Educational Aesthetics: Replaced developer SaaS styling (dark neon cards, terminal command prompt, and large numeric stats) with a warm paper canvas, sumi ink typography, and an annotated syllabus flow.
  • Editorial Typography: Integrated classic serif display fonts (Charter / Georgia) with comfortable reading margins and open line spacing.
  • Syllabus Architecture: Formatted the 4-Stage Guru Funnel into an annotated field guide layout with distinct narrative steps.

---

[1.0.0] - 2026-11-03

Added

  • Project Genesis: Initialized using VanillaSSG zero-hydration static site architecture.
  • Investigative Landing Page: Designed homepage introducing the 4-stage funnel model, recent dossier highlights, and a consumer red-flags checklist.
  • Investigative Case Studies:
  • iman-gadzhi.md: Detailed case study on the pivot from SMMA agency blueprints to AI challenges, micro-upsells, and high-ticket mastermind packages.
  • tai-lopez.md: Blueprint analysis tracking the archetype from the garage Lamborghini campaign to crypto, NFTs, and retail liquidations.
  • anatomy-of-a-guru-funnel.md: Comprehensive guide explaining status intimidation, shovel-selling, and blame deflection.
  • the-pivot-cycle.md: Analysis documenting historical product exhaustion cycles from 90s newspaper ads to modern AI bootcamps.
  • About & Methodology: Added comprehensive about.html explaining primary source verification, regulatory filings cross-referencing, and the legal gray-zone framework.
  • Automated OpenGraph Cards: Generated vector SVG social cards and 256x256 compact side-by-side thumbnail previews.
  • SEO & Search Architecture: Dynamic XML sitemap, RSS feed, robots.txt, and automated metadata synchronization.
  • Project Documentation: Created BRAIN.md, RULES.md, and SCOPE.md capturing architectural principles and roadmap boundaries.
