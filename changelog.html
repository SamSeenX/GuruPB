[1.0.0] - 2026-11-03

Added

  • Project Genesis: Initialized GuruPB using VanillaSSG zero-hydration static site architecture.
  • Investigative Landing Page: Designed high-contrast homepage introducing the 4-stage funnel model (The Hook, The Shovel, The Trap, The Shield), recent dossier highlights, and a consumer red-flags checklist.
  • Investigative Case Studies:
  • iman-gadzhi.md: Detailed case study on the pivot from SMMA agency blueprints to AI challenges, micro-upsells, and high-ticket mastermind packages.
  • tai-lopez.md: Blueprint analysis tracking the archetype from the garage Lamborghini campaign to crypto, NFTs, and retail liquidations.
  • anatomy-of-a-guru-funnel.md: Comprehensive guide explaining status intimidation, shovel-selling, and blame deflection.
  • the-pivot-cycle.md: Analysis documenting historical product exhaustion cycles from 90s newspaper ads to modern AI bootcamps.
  • About & Methodology: Added comprehensive about.html explaining primary source verification, regulatory filings cross-referencing, and the legal gray-zone framework.
  • Automated OpenGraph Cards: Generated vector SVG social cards and 256x256 compact side-by-side thumbnail previews.
  • SEO & Search Architecture: Dynamic XML sitemap, RSS feed, robots.txt, and automated metadata synchronization.
  • Project Documentation: Created BRAIN.md, RULES.md, and SCOPE.md capturing architectural principles and roadmap boundaries.
